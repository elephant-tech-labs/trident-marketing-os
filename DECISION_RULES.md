# Decision Rules

## Global Messaging Rules

- Clarity over cleverness.
- Utility over fluff.
- Timing over volume.
- Avoid over-promising.
- Avoid making customers feel judged or inactive.

## Agent Campaign Rules

Do not use:
- "It has been 90 days"
- "We haven't heard from you"
- "Do you have any deals?"
- "Are you still active?"
- Portal/dashboard/login language until the portal is ready.

Use:
- "Keep Trident handy"
- "When inspection timing matters"
- "For inspection availability, specialty services, or client questions"
- "One team, fewer moving parts"

## Homeowner Campaign Rules

Use:
- Prior inspection trust
- Property address when helpful
- Practical next steps
- Clear CTAs

Avoid:
- Excessive fear
- Claims that assume closing date unless data supports it
- Claims that every issue requires urgent action

## Termite/WDO Rules

Use:
- "If your report included findings..."
- "Treatment, repair, prevention, or documentation"
- "Because Trident already has the report context..."

Avoid:
- "Your closing is close"
- "Your lender will not fund"
- "Action needed" unless verified for that exact contact.
