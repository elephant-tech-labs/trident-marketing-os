# Consent and Compliance

## Email

All email campaigns must respect:
- Email Opt-Out
- Do Not Market
- Unsubscribe link
- Physical business address in footer

## SMS

All SMS campaigns must respect:
- SMS Opt-Out
- STOP replies
- Business-hour send windows
- Required opt-out language where applicable

Recommended SMS opt-out:
`Reply STOP to opt out.`
