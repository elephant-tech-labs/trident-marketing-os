# KPI Dashboard Spec

## Agent Campaign KPIs

Primary:
- Agent replies
- Calls/texts
- Reactivated inspection referrals
- First inspection after reactivation
- BD task completion rate

Secondary:
- Email opens
- CTA clicks
- SMS replies
- Unsubscribes

## Termite Campaign KPIs

Primary:
- Quote form submissions
- Calls/texts
- Treatment bookings
- Treatment revenue
- Clearance/documentation completions

Secondary:
- Email opens
- Email clicks
- SMS replies
- Unsubscribes
- STOP rate
