# TIG | Post-Report Pest Enrollment P1 - Recent Inspection Clients

This workflow begins after a client's home inspection report is published. It uses the recent inspection relationship as the trust anchor and introduces general pest service without referencing report findings.

## Strategic Role

The first two messages provide report support. Pest enrollment begins on Day 3, while Trident still has a relationship advantage and before the homeowner is likely to receive broader new-home marketing.

The campaign must feel:

- recognizable and personal
- calm and useful
- professional and human
- relevant to the inspected property
- optional rather than urgent

## Campaign Structure

- 1 report-support email
- 1 report-support SMS
- 3 pest enrollment emails
- 4 pest enrollment SMS messages
- Pest enrollment begins on Day 3 after report publication
- The dedicated sequence ends on Day 21

## Offer

If the client chooses to start pest service, the first service is complimentary.

Do not describe the pest check itself as complimentary unless Trident confirms that the check is free without enrollment.

## Primary Action

`Book My Pest Check`

## Reply Action

`Reply PEST or call/text 949-294-1188`

## Critical Guardrails

- Do not reference report findings during this workflow.
- Do not mention termites, WDO, rodents, infestation, treatment, pricing, a plan name, or a contract.
- Do not assume the transaction has closed or call the property the client's new home.
- Do not use scarcity, reserved-slot, last-chance, or fear language.
- Delay issue-specific follow-up until at least Day 30 and keep it in a separate workflow.
- Send SMS only to contacts with valid SMS consent.

## Design Direction

The email design is a quiet, letter-style continuation of the inspection experience. It uses one logo, one inspection-context block, one primary CTA, and a clear reply/call path. It intentionally avoids promotional pest imagery, decorative panels, emojis, gradients, and competing calls to action.
