# Timeline

Timing is anchored to `Report Published At`, using the property's local time zone.

| Step | Timing | Channel | Asset | Purpose |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Day 1, 10:00 AM | Email | Support E1 | Offer report help with no pest message |
| 2 | Day 2, 11:30 AM | SMS | Support S1 | Establish an easy human reply path |
| 3 | Day 3, 10:00 AM | Email | Pest E1 | Introduce pest service as an optional next step |
| 4 | Day 5, 12:00 PM | SMS | Pest S1 | Create a low-friction PEST reply opportunity |
| 5 | Day 8, 10:00 AM | Email | Pest E2 | Explain what a general pest check covers |
| 6 | Day 10, 12:00 PM | SMS | Pest S2 | Reinforce practical scope |
| 7 | Day 15, 12:00 PM | SMS | Pest S3 | Reinforce familiar-team continuity |
| 8 | Day 20, 10:00 AM | Email | Pest E3 | Close the email sequence respectfully |
| 9 | Day 21, 12:00 PM | SMS | Pest S4 | Final light reply invitation |

## Timing Rules

- Do not place a pest offer in report delivery.
- Start pest enrollment on Day 3.
- Do not send two workflow messages on the same day.
- Move messages that fall outside approved sending windows to the next permitted local-time window.
- Exit immediately after interest, a question, booking, human follow-up, opt-out, complaint, or existing pest service is detected.
