# Timeline

Recommended launch cadence for 2025 inspection clients.

| Step | Timing |  Best Send Time PT | Channel | Asset                                               | Purpose                                                            |
| ---: | ------ | -----------------: | ------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|    1 | Day 0  |    Tuesday 9:10 AM | Email   | E1 - Seasonal Pest Check After Your 2025 Inspection | Introduce timely pest check based on prior inspection relationship |
|    2 | Day 1  | Wednesday 12:15 PM | SMS     | S1 - Quick Pest Check Follow-Up                     | Make reply/call/text easy                                          |
|    3 | Day 5  |     Monday 9:10 AM | Email   | E2 - What The Pest Check Covers                     | Explain what gets checked and reduce friction                      |
|    4 | Day 8  |   Thursday 2:15 PM | SMS     | S2 - Pest Check Reminder                            | Light reminder with reply keyword                                  |
|    5 | Day 14 |  Wednesday 9:10 AM | Email   | E3 - Final Seasonal Pest Check Reminder             | Final helpful reminder and transition to homeowner nurture         |

## Timing Notes

Start on Tuesday where possible.

Avoid launching on Friday, Saturday, Sunday, or Monday.

Use a shorter sequence than the Pre-2025 pest workflow because this audience is warmer and more recent.
