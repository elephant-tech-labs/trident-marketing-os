## Copy Direction

Use:

* prior inspection trust
* seasonal timing
* common entry points
* moisture zones
* foundation lines
* vents
* crawl access
* garage/exterior gaps
* complimentary first pest check/service if they choose to start protection

Avoid:

* scare tactics
* implying an active infestation
* overly strong scarcity
* “last call” until the final email, and even then keep it soft
