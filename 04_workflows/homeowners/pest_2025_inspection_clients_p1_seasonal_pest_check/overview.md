## Positioning

This campaign should not feel like a hard reactivation campaign. These are more recent clients, so the message should feel like a timely property-care follow-up after their 2025 inspection.

Use this positioning:

> We inspected your home last year. As we move through another pest season, this is a good time to check the common entry points and pest-risk areas around the home.

## Offer

If the homeowner chooses to start pest protection, the first pest check/service is complimentary.

## Avoid

* Fear-based pest language
* Claims that a pest issue exists unless confirmed
* Overly aggressive urgency
* “Last call” too early in the sequence
* Too many SMS touches
