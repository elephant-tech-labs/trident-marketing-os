# Positioning

## Company Positioning

Trident is not just an inspection company. Trident is a property-services partner that helps agents and homeowners reduce friction, understand next steps, and coordinate related property services through one team.

## Agent Positioning

Trident is:
- A deal-smoothing partner
- A workflow advantage
- A risk-reduction system
- A calm, professional extension of the agent

## Homeowner Positioning

Trident is:
- A trusted inspection and property-care partner
- A practical next-step guide after inspection findings
- A provider for pest, termite, and ongoing home protection needs
