# Trident Marketing OS - Master Context

Trident Inspection Group is a Southern California property services company serving Orange, Los Angeles, Riverside, and San Bernardino counties.

## Core Services

- Residential inspections
- Commercial inspections
- Sewer inspections
- Mold inspections
- Chimney inspections
- Thermal imaging
- Pest control
- Termite/WDO inspections and treatment
- Rodent exclusion
- HOA services

## Primary Audiences

### Agents / Realtors

Goal:
Make Trident memorable, easy to reach, and trusted when inspection timing matters.

Current positioning:
- No agent portal language until portal is ready.
- Use one-call / one-text positioning.
- Emphasize fast practical scheduling.
- Emphasize fewer moving parts.
- Emphasize client experience.
- Avoid language that makes agents feel inactive, slow, or judged.

### Homeowners / Property Owners

Goal:
Convert one-time inspection and service clients into repeat services, pest plans, termite treatment, seasonal property care, reviews, and referrals.

Current positioning:
- Use prior inspection trust.
- Use address/date personalization carefully.
- Offer practical next steps.
- Avoid excessive fear-based messaging.
